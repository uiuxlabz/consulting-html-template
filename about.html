Our story

From deck to delivery

CONSULT was founded by three partners who had each led a turnaround, scaled a team from 30 to 400, and shipped a product under real constraints. We kept seeing the same pattern: brilliant strategy, zero traction.

The fix was not a better deck — it was a tighter loop between diagnosis, choice, and delivery. That loop became our method: Diagnose → Design → Deliver, with proof every Friday.

Approach

Diagnose → Design → Deliver

We move from evidence to choices to shipped outcomes. Working sessions replace status decks, and a weekly demo replaces the quarterly review.

1 Diagnose

Find the real constraint

Interviews, unit economics, pipeline and delivery data. One candid readout. One constraint named.

2 Design

Make the choice explicit

2–3 options with economics priced and trade-offs named. You choose. We turn it into a 90-day plan.

3 Deliver

Ship every week

Embedded sprints. Demo on Friday. Metrics that move — not slideware that impresses.
